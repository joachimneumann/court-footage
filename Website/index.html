Instant replay for court sports.

Court Footage is an iPhone app for saving your own highlights from pickleball, tennis, padel, and other court sports. Mount your phone courtside, then tap after a point to save the rally to Photos.

Free. No in-app purchases. Github

Android app: not yet implemented

  1. Set up courtside

    Mount your iPhone in portrait or landscape. Open Court Footage and use the live camera to frame the court.

  2. Tap after the point

    When a rally is worth keeping, run to the phone and tap the center of the screen. And continue playing. The app automatically saves the footage to your Photos.

  3. Watch it back

    You can watch the saved replay immediately in the Court Footage app, but it is also saved to your photos.

Choose what you keep.

Clips up to 1 hour
Choose a length starting at 5 seconds and skip up to 10 seconds just before the tap.
Up to 4K
Choose 720p, 1080p, or 4K when your camera supports it. See the estimated clip size before recording.
Optional court audio
Choose whether your clips include the sounds of play.

Your footage stays with you.

Court Footage does not upload your data or footage anywhere. When you save a clip to Photos, any syncing follows your Photos and iCloud settings.
