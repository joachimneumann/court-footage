# Court Footage privacy policy

**The Court Footage app does not connect to the internet, upload any information, or track you.**

Court Footage starts using your iPhone’s camera when you open the app with Camera access and permission to add clips to Photos. It uses the microphone if you enable audio and allow Microphone access. It keeps recent footage and any audio in a temporary buffer on your iPhone so you can save clips of play. Footage you haven’t selected is discarded as it leaves the buffer or when recording ends.

When you tap to save, Court Footage creates a temporary video file and asks Photos to add the clip to your library. The app requests permission to add videos to Photos and does not read other items in your library. After saving, the app keeps the temporary copy so you can open the clip while the next buffer fills. It removes the copy once that buffer is full and you have closed any open replay. The app removes failed exports and clears any leftover copies the next time it opens. You can manage saved clips in Photos. Any syncing of those clips follows your Photos and iCloud settings. If you open the online privacy policy from the app, it opens in your browser.

Court Footage processes recordings on your iPhone and stores your recording preferences locally. It does not send recordings or personal information to its developer. The app has no accounts, advertising, or analytics. You can change access to the camera, microphone, or Photos in iPhone Settings.
