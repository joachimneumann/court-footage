Court Footage turns your iPhone into an instant replay camera for pickleball, tennis, padel, and other court sports.

Mount your iPhone courtside in portrait or landscape and open the app. Court Footage keeps the most recent footage as you play. After a point worth keeping, tap the camera preview to save the preceding clip to Photos. You can watch the replay in Court Footage while the camera keeps recording.

• Choose a clip length from 5 seconds to 1 hour. Skip up to 10 seconds just before your tap to leave out the walk back to the phone.
• Use the front or rear camera. Pinch to zoom or tap a zoom preset to frame the court.
• Choose 720p, 1080p, or 4K where supported by your camera. Turn court audio on or off.

Court Footage works offline. The app processes footage on your iPhone and does not track you. Court Footage does not upload your data or footage anywhere. When you save a clip to Photos, any syncing follows your Photos and iCloud settings. Court Footage is open source under the MIT License.
